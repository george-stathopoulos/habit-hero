# Playful art direction

Updated 2026-10-17 following the user's correction of the first preview.

The first Playful phone in the supplied visual blueprint is the style reference. The intended audience is approximately ages 5–8. Characters should look like approachable children in a bright animated picture book.

## Characters

- Large rounded heads, expressive eyes, rosy cheeks, compact bodies and short sturdy limbs: approximately three heads tall.
- Reconstruct the actual mockup outfits: girl with pink long-sleeved tunic, gold belt, full purple leggings, pink boots and cape; boy with the navy/grey adventurer outfit, full navy trousers, brown boots and blue cape. Do not invent new hoodie/overall designs.
- Preserve the mockup poses, faces and hair. The girl has the Playful white kitten; the boy has the small blue dragon shown in his reference.
- Simple soft materials and shapes. Avoid realistic adult proportions, fashion poses, elaborate fitted fantasy outfits, exposed-leg costume styling or ornamental adult fantasy styling. Boots should match the simple mockup boots.
- Girl and boy appearances must belong to the same age group and visual family.

## World

- Rounded pastel castle, puffy clouds, simplified hills, playful trees and oversized friendly flowers.
- Soft toy-like dimensional shading and clear silhouettes rather than realistic scenery or ornate fantasy painting.
- Keep separate transparent hero and foreground layers. Near flowers overlap the hero's lower edge; the head, face and greeting remain clear.
- Preserve the implemented independent parallax coefficients and reduced-motion behavior.

## Task illustrations

Use the same softly rounded picture-book treatment for the bed, reading nook and toothbrush scenes. The images should feel welcoming and simple at phone size, with matching colors and recognizable large objects.

The original mature-looking preview artwork is superseded. The mockup is the identity reference, not just inspiration. Keep the faces, proportions, hair, outfits and companions as close as the source permits. Pixel-identical source artwork requires the original separate assets; generated reconstruction must not be described as an exact extraction. Do not use the mature preview as a reference for later themes or new poses.

## Activity icons

Habit and activity rows use colourful rounded vector badges, not miniature scene images. Keep bed, toothbrush, book and outdoor icons immediately recognizable, with soft dimensional shading and the mockup’s Playful palette. Large scene illustrations belong on mission detail screens.

## Background movement

Every theme should have continuous gentle ambient movement that fits its scenery. Playful uses drifting cloud layers and subtle daylight changes. A waterfall scene should animate its water; a night sky should animate its stars. Idle movement belongs to the background: do not bob the characters, foliage, habit icons or controls. Keep user-driven multiplane parallax. Pause ambient motion when Home or the page is not visible, and respect reduced motion.

The magic egg may wobble and glow as a progression object, per the user’s later request. Scrolling can close a Playful leaf/flower canopy over the world. These effects must remain reversible and respect reduced motion. Larger screens should use the available width for scene/content columns rather than displaying a narrow phone frame.
